AirQR Architecture

Zero-Cloud Footprint Guarantee

100% In-Memory Data

No data is ever persisted to disk, cloud databases, or relay storage. All bytes are piped through ephemeral browser memory.

Peer-to-Peer Encryption

WebRTC DataChannel operates over SCTP encapsulated in DTLS. Data is encrypted end-to-end between your browser and your phone.

16KB Binary Slicing & Backpressure

Files up to 50MB are sliced into 16KB windows with bufferedAmount monitoring (<64KB) to prevent browser buffer congestion.
